# Student Data Notice

_How to keep the children you teach safe in your journal._

Dawnslate never receives your journal, so you are in charge of the information you keep about the people you teach. These are good habits, and in many places they are the law (for example the GDPR and UK GDPR, COPPA and FERPA in the US, and school safeguarding policies).

## Before you record

- Tell parents or guardians that you keep lesson notes and send summaries, and get their agreement where your rules require it.
- Record only what helps you teach: attendance, what you covered, homework, progress.
- Avoid sensitive details (health, behaviour incidents, family matters) unless you truly need them and are allowed to keep them.

## Keeping it safe

- Use a passcode on your device and turn on Dawnslate’s app lock.
- Turn on “Hide in app switcher” if others can see your screen.
- Store exported backups only in places you control; they are readable files.

## Sharing

- A parent card is about one student. Send it only to that student’s parent or guardian.
- Check the recipient before you tap send in WhatsApp, Telegram, WeChat or Messages.
- Group lessons: each parent gets their own card; other children’s names are not included.

## When a student leaves

- Remove the student (Students › the student › Edit › Remove student) when you no longer need their records, and delete old backups that hold them.
- If a parent asks, show them what you hold, correct it, export it for them, or delete it.
